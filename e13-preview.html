About this site

What this is

Pick My Costume is a free Halloween costume picker. Answer a few quick questions and get three costume ideas, for yourself, your kid, your partner, your group, or the whole family.

Your answers score a hand-picked list of 75 ideas. Your vibe and what you're into count double; effort level and the details tune the rest. No randomness, no account, no email.

Your quiz answers never leave your phone. We only track anonymous usage stats.

Then what? On your results, tap “This is my pick” on a costume to save it and get your AI costume plan.

Questions or a costume we should add? Email hello@pickmycostume.com.

Halloween by the numbers: Americans are expected to spend $13.5 billion on Halloween in 2026, and half of everyone celebrating dresses in costume. Nearly half of shoppers use AI to brainstorm costume ideas. (Source: NRF's 2026 Halloween survey.) Google's Frightgeist tracks the most-searched costumes each October. Ideas marked Trending ride a top-ranked costume lane in NRF's 2026 survey — each card shows its stat and source.
