Privacy

Effective: September 28, 2026

Pick My Costume is a free Halloween costume picker with no account, no login, and no email required. This policy explains what we collect and what we don't.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage counts. We count events like quiz completions and button taps so we can see which parts of the site work. They carry a random ID, not your name, email, or account. Nothing that identifies you.
  • Your email, only if you ask. Tapping “Email me this plan” sends your address to our server for the sole purpose of sending that one email with your costume plan.
  • Oct 27 reminder (only if you tick the box). If you also tick “Remind me Oct 27,” we keep your address until October 27, 2026, to send you one reminder email that morning with last-minute ideas you can make that night. That is it: one plan email, plus one reminder only if you asked for it. We delete reminder addresses right after October 27. We never sell your address, never add it to any other list, and never use it for anything you did not ask for. Want it deleted sooner? Email hello@pickmycostume.com and a human will remove it.
  • Typed costume names (structural only). If you type a custom costume name, the text itself never leaves your device; we only record that the feature was used.
  • Costume searches (anonymous). When you search for a costume, we log the first 30 characters of your search term with a random ID, never your name, so we can see which costumes people look for and add the ones we are missing.

What we don't collect

  • Your individual quiz answers stay in your browser. We record which costume ideas get shown and picked, in aggregate, without tying them to you.
  • We don't ask for, and don't store, names, phone numbers, or locations.
  • No ads. Analytics goes only to PostHog, our own analytics provider, to count usage. Never sold or shared with advertisers.

How long we keep things

Anonymous usage counts are kept as long as they are useful for improving the site. Email addresses for the Oct 27 reminder are deleted right after October 27, 2026. If you ask us to delete your address sooner, we will.

AI assistants and connectors

If you use Pick My Costume through an AI assistant (like ChatGPT or Claude), the connector only reads our public costume guides, the same pages anyone can open. It does not log your questions or store anything about you.

Cookies

The site may store small values in your browser's local storage so the quiz remembers where you left off. Nothing in local storage is sent anywhere.

Children

The site is for parents and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13; email features (the plan email and the optional Oct 27 reminder) are for adults.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the site: hello@pickmycostume.com.
