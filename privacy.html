Privacy

Effective: September 28, 2026

Pick My Costume is a free Halloween costume picker with no account, no login, and no email required. This policy explains what we collect and what we don't.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage counts. We count events like quiz completions and button taps so we can see which parts of the site work. These counts are not tied to you — no name, email, device ID, or IP is stored with them.
  • Typed costume names (structural only). If you type a custom costume name, the text itself never leaves your device; we only record that the feature was used.

What we don't collect

  • Your quiz answers stay in your browser's memory and are never sent to us.
  • We don't ask for, and don't store, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or locations.
  • No ads, no trackers sold or shared with advertisers.

Cookies

The site may store small values in your browser's local storage so the quiz remembers where you left off. Nothing in local storage is sent anywhere.

Children

Parents use this site with their kids in mind, so we built it as if every visitor is a child: nothing personal is collected from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the site: hello@pickmycostume.com.
