What Can I Be for Halloween With a Black Dress?

Short answer: a black dress plus one strong signal. The dress alone reads as a dress. Add two braids and pale makeup and it reads deadpan diva in 15 minutes. Add black tights and braids and it is the goth braids look in 20. A long black gown pairs up into a goth couple in 30, and the same closet turns wicked as the bad witch in a good witch, bad witch duo in 20. Every build below starts from a black dress you already own, lists the real hands-on time and what else you need, and links to the full step-by-step guide.

Questions, answered

What is the fastest black dress costume here?

The Deadpan Diva at 15 minutes of hands-on time: black dress, two braids, pale makeup, done. The goth braids look takes 20, the duo 20, and the goth couple 30.

Will a black dress on its own read as a costume?

No. On its own it reads as a dress. Each build here adds one loud signal on top: braids and pale makeup, a streaked wig and neck bolts, or green face paint. The signal is the costume.

Do I need a special kind of black dress?

No. The diva and braids builds use a plain black dress you own. The goth couple uses a long black dress or gown, and thrifted is fine. The bad witch half of the duo works with any black dress or set of black clothes.

Are any of these black dress costumes for two people?

Two of them. The Macabre Goth Couple is built for two, and Good Witch, Bad Witch pairs your black dress with a partner in pink.
