The 5-minute build: Crowd Camouflage

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Crowd Camouflage

Real numbers: 5 min - $2-5 - Easy

You will need:

  1. Gray hoodie, 1 (own)
  2. Dark pants, 1 pair (own)
  3. Gray beanie, 1 (own, or buy: dollar store)
  4. Blank adhesive name tag, 1 (buy: dollar store)
  5. Pen, 1 (own)
  1. Pull on the gray hoodie with the hood up, the dark pants, and the gray beanie.
  2. Leave the blank adhesive name tag blank, that IS the joke. Write nothing on it with the pen and stick it to the chest of the gray hoodie.
  3. The one thing that ruins this costume is filling in the tag.
  4. Keep a blank expression, hood up, and blend into the background of every group photo.
  5. Optional pro finish: stand in the back row of every group photo with a perfectly blank expression. The costume only works if you commit to being forgettable, ham it up by being boring.
