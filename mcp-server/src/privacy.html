Privacy Policy - Pick My Costume assistant tools

Effective date: September 25, 2026

The short version

These tools collect nothing about you. No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no emails, no tracking. They answer your costume questions from a fixed bank of 138 original costume ideas, and what you ask is processed in memory and then forgotten.

What this is

Pick My Costume offers read-only helper tools through AI assistants (such as ChatGPT plugins and Claude connectors). They recommend Halloween costume ideas and show step-by-step build instructions from pickmycostume.com.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

  • No accounts, no sign-in, and no names, emails, or contact details are requested or stored.
  • The questions you type (for example, "toddler ghost costume") are processed in memory to return an answer. They are not logged, not retained, and not tied to you.
  • The tool server sets no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no analytics identifiers.
  • We do not sell, share, or disclose any data, because there is no data to share.

Links to our website

Tool responses link to costume guides on pickmycostume.com. If you open those links, the website's own practices apply. The site has no accounts and runs no ads. It does collect anonymous usage events through PostHog analytics, for example that a quiz was started, a costume idea was saved, or a button was tapped. These events carry no names, emails, or quiz answers, and the tools themselves set no cookies or tracking identifiers.

Children

The tools provide general costume craft instructions for parents and guardians. They are not directed at children under 13, and they collect no information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page before the change takes effect.
