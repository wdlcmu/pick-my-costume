Couples Halloween Costume Ideas

The best couples costumes have a joke a stranger gets in three seconds: ketchup and mustard, plug and socket, sun and moon. Pick the one where both people are equally recognizable, nobody wants to be the human accessory. Build both halves side by side so the colors and sizes match, and agree in advance on the effort level, one elaborate half next to one thrown-together half reads as a mistake, not a duo. Every pair below links to full build guides with real hands-on time and honest cost, so you both know what the evening is going to take.
