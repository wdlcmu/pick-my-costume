# Privacy Policy : Pick My Costume assistant tools

Effective date: September 28, 2026

## The short version

These tools collect nothing about you. No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no
emails, no tracking. They answer your costume questions from a fixed bank of
169 original costume ideas, and what you ask is processed in memory and then
forgotten.

## What this is

Pick My Costume offers read-only helper tools through AI assistants (such as
ChatGPT plugins and Claude connectors). They recommend Halloween costume ideas
and show step-by-step build instructions from
[pickmycostume.com](https://pickmycostume.com).

## Data we collect

**None.** Specifically:

- No accounts, no sign-in, and no names, emails, or contact details are
  requested or stored.
- The questions you type (for example, "toddler ghost costume") are processed
  in memory to return an answer. They are not logged, not retained, and not
  tied to you.
- The tool server sets no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no analytics
  identifiers.
- We do not sell, share, or disclose any data, because there is no data to
  share.

## Data retention

Nothing to retain. Because the tools collect no data, there are no logs, no
user profiles, and no stored history to keep or delete. Each request is
processed in memory and forgotten when it completes.

## Your controls

There is nothing to opt out of and nothing to delete, because nothing about
you is collected. If you ever believe we hold something about you, write to
hello@pickmycostume.com and we will answer within 30 days.

## Links to our website

Tool responses link to costume guides on pickmycostume.com. If you open those
links, the website's own practices apply. The site has no accounts and runs no
ads. It does collect anonymous usage events through PostHog analytics, for
example that a quiz was started, a costume idea was saved, or a button was
tapped. These events carry no names, emails, or quiz answers, and the tools
themselves set no cookies or tracking identifiers.

## Children

The tools provide general costume craft instructions for parents and guardians.
They are not directed at children under 13, and they collect no information
from anyone of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page
before the change takes effect.
