# Terms of Use : Pick My Costume assistant tools

Effective date: September 25, 2026

## The short version

These are free tools that suggest Halloween costume ideas and show you how to
make them from stuff you may already own. Use them normally, follow the safety
notes in the guides, and use common sense when building.

## What the tools do

Pick My Costume's assistant tools recommend from a bank of 169 original,
trademark-free costume ideas. For each idea they can show a materials list,
step-by-step instructions, estimated time and cost, and safety notes. The tools
are read-only: they give you information and change nothing.

The tools are free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, and no purchases.

## Safety

The build guides are general craft instructions, not professional advice. The
safety notes in each guide reflect common-sense precautions (for example,
having an adult handle sharp cutting tools and keeping small pieces away from
toddlers), but no guide can cover every situation. By following a guide you
accept that building a costume involves some ordinary craft risk, and you agree
to use your own judgment. If something in a guide looks unsafe for your
situation, skip it or adapt it.
