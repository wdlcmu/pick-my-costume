❓ How it works

We take your ZIP, find your town, and ask Open-Meteo for the temperature on recent October 31 evenings, 5pm to 8pm local time. Closer to Halloween (inside 16 days), we switch to the real forecast automatically.

The cozy shortlist is built from our real costume bank: ideas whose build plans are made for layers (hoodies, robes, sweats, jackets). Every pick links to its free step-by-step guide.

Weather data: Open-Meteo (free, no key). Costume ideas: the real Pick My Costume bank.
