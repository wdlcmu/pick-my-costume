Questions, answered

How do you size one theme for different ages?

Build each kid's piece in their own size from the same colors and materials. The guides size per wearer, so a seven-year-old's dino and a three-year-old's dino match without either one swimming in it.

What works for three or more siblings?

The Chipmunk Trio, the Dino Herd, and the Ice Kingdom Crew are written for groups. Each kid gets a color or a role, and extra kids slot in without changing the theme.

What if one sibling wants scary and one wants cute?

Try the Witchy Sister Trio: it is built on contrast, one dark and one bright. Cat & Mouse also pairs a chaser and a runner, so different moods still match.

Can twins wear the exact same costume?

Yes. Salt & Pepper and Ketchup & Mustard are true pairs with mirrored builds. Make both at once from one supply run, and the two builds take less time than two separate costumes.
