Common questions

I left my costume to the last minute. What can I still make?

This page filters our 169-idea costume bank by how many days are left until Halloween, using each costume's real build time. With one to three days left, it only shows easy builds of 30 minutes or less with no drying time.

Where do the build times come from?

Every time, cost, and effort label on this page is the one attached to that costume's step-by-step build guide in our own costume bank. Nothing is estimated for this page.

Can I make one of these tonight?

Costumes marked Make tonight take 30 minutes or less, are rated easy, and need no drying time. Each one links to its full build guide.
