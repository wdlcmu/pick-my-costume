📸 The porch photo

The pairing is the photo. Four rules for the shot that gets texted to the grandparents:

  • Put the pumpkin at the kid's feet, not behind them, so both stay in focus.
  • Light the pumpkin 10 minutes before the photo so the candle glow shows in the shot.
  • Shoot at dusk with the porch light off for the spooky glow.
  • Match the costume to the pumpkin face: the joke only lands when they rhyme.

❓ Pumpkin carving questions

How long does a carved pumpkin last?

About 5 to 7 days in cool weather. Carve the weekend before Halloween so it still looks fresh on the big night.

What tools do we need?

A serrated pumpkin saw (or a sturdy kitchen knife for adults only), a big spoon for scooping, and a marker to draw the design first. Kids draw, adults cut.

Can toddlers help carve?

Yes, with the safe jobs: they draw the face with marker, scoop with a spoon, and drop the battery tea light in. No blades for little hands.

What if the pumpkin rots before Halloween?

Keep it in the shade during the day and bring it inside on warm afternoons. Or carve a foam pumpkin from the craft store: it lasts for years and the designs above work the same.
