Most costume guides quietly assume one body. These do not.

Each build lists what you need and links to a full guide with real hands-on time. No size charts, no squeezing.

Questions, answered

How do I make sure a DIY costume fits?

Measure the wearer, not a size chart. The guides size each piece to the person: sheet length from shoulder to ankle, board width across the shoulders. Cut big and trim; you cannot add fabric back.

Which costumes are the most forgiving?

The Classic Ghost, the Classic Wizard, and the Garden Gnome. All three drape from the shoulders with a belt or sash to set the shape, so they fit the same way at any size.

Are the food costumes comfortable to sit in?

The Donut and the Pizza Slice hang from shoulder straps and leave your back free, so sitting and driving work. The Coffee Cup is a tube; pick it for standing parties, not long car rides.

Do I need special plus-size supplies?

No. You may need a bigger sheet or board for the same build, and the guides say where. Everything else is the standard craft list.
