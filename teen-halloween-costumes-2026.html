The full build: Deadpan Diva

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Deadpan Diva

Real numbers: 15 min - $3-8 - Easy

You will need:

  1. Black dress, 1 (own)
  2. Very pale foundation or white non-toxic face paint, 1 (buy: toy store)
  3. Dark eyeliner and lipstick, 1 set (own, or buy: dollar store)
  4. 2 hair ties (own)
  1. Braid the hair into two tight braids with the hair ties.
  2. Buy the white non-toxic face paint if needed.
  3. Test the pale foundation on the jawline first and blend fully, patchy pale makeup reads as sick, not deadpan.
  4. Apply the pale foundation evenly over the face, blending into the neck so there is no mask line.
  5. Add the dark eyeliner and lipstick for contrast.
  6. Put on the black dress, fold the arms, and hold the stare.
  7. Use skin-safe makeup and wash it all off before bed.
  8. Optional pro finish: add a thin black ribbon choker. The choker plus the braids plus the stare is the complete silhouette, without it, it is just a black dress.

FAQ

What is the fastest teen costume that still looks intentional?

Crowd Camouflage: 5 minutes, $2 to $5, gray hoodie and dark pants. Error 404 is 10 minutes if you have a printer.

How do I make a scary costume that is not gory?

Deadpan Diva and the Classic Vampire read scary through silhouette and makeup, not blood. Both build from closet clothes.

Will these survive a whole night out?

The closet-based ones will: there is almost nothing to fall off. The Pickle has glued parts, so give it the full drying time before the party.

How much should a teen costume cost?

The teen picks on this page run $0 to $12: Error 404 is $0 to $5, Crowd Camouflage $2 to $5, Deadpan Diva $3 to $8, the Classic Vampire $5 to $10, Pickle $3 to $8.
