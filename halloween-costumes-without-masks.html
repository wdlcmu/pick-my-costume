Questions, answered

Why do schools ban masks?

Teachers need to see faces, and masks block side vision on stairs and in crowds. These costumes keep the face fully open, so they pass most school rules. Check your school letter to be sure.

Do any of these need face paint?

No. Face paint is optional on a few and required on none. The Ballerina, the Cheerleader, and the Referee read from the outfit and the props alone.

Will these work with glasses?

Yes. Nothing sits on the face, so glasses stay on all night. The Pirate Captain and the Referee read fine with the wearer's own glasses.

Are these good for toddlers?

The Tiny Firefighter, the Little Baker, and the Ballerina are built for toddlers: hats come off fast, nothing covers the mouth or nose, and bathroom breaks stay simple.
