Last-Minute Costume Ideas

Quick answer: Classic Ghost (10 min), Emoji Crew (15 min), Basketball Star (5 min + 10 min drying) — all buildable tonight in 30 minutes or less of hands-on work. Tap any name for its full build guide: materials list, numbered steps, and safety notes.

It is the night of the party and you have nothing. Every costume below takes 30 minutes or less of hands-on work, and total time including drying stays under two hours, so you can start after dinner and finish before bed. Drying time is shown honestly on every card. The two marked No shopping need nothing beyond basic household items; the rest may need a quick store run. Have more than the basics at home? Questions, answered

What is the one rule for last-minute costumes?

One hero piece. A ghost needs only the sheet, a tourist needs only the camera and socks-with-sandals. Build around a single recognizable item and let closet clothes do the rest.

What should I skip when time is short?

Anything with drying time: paint, glue-heavy builds, papier-mache. A hair dryer buys you minutes, not miracles.

How do I not look like I gave up?

Commit to the bit. A name tag, a prop, or a one-line character voice sells a simple costume more than extra accessories do.
