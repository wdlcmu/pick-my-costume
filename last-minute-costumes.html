Questions, answered

What is the one rule for last-minute costumes?

One hero piece. A ghost needs only the sheet, a tourist needs only the camera and socks-with-sandals. Build around a single recognizable item and let closet clothes do the rest.

What should I skip when time is short?

Anything that has to dry: paint, glue-heavy builds, papier-mache. A hair dryer buys you minutes, not miracles.

How do I not look like I gave up?

Commit to the bit. A name tag, a prop, or a one-line character voice sells a simple costume more than extra accessories do.
