Classroom costume swap: starter kit

A free starter kit for running a classroom Halloween costume swap. Print the door sign, the steps, the rules, the size bin labels, the per-costume rack cards, the rack-slot sign-up sheet, and the tear-off slips; every QR opens the costume browse shelf, measured as ?src=share-swaprack. The live rack tracker at section 8 keeps its board in the page address. Nothing here is indexed by search engines; it is a print utility, not a landing page.

2. How it works

Five steps, readable from across the room.

PICK MY COSTUME

  1. Wash and bag it

    Only clean costumes go on the rack. Wash it, let it dry, and put it in a bag with the size written on the outside.

  2. Pin a size label on it

    Every item needs a size you can read from three feet away: a tag, a piece of tape, a safety pin note. No guessing.

  3. Fill out a swap slip

    Write what you are giving (size and costume) and what you are looking for. Clip it to the hanger or drop it in the box.

  4. Take only what fits

    One in, one out. Try it on your kid before you leave the rack if you can. Drop your slip in the box so the teacher can match sizes.

  5. Return what does not work

    Wrong size, wrong vibe, kid changed their mind: bring it back to the rack so the next family can grab it.

3. Swap-day rules

Post these next to the rack.

PICK MY COSTUME

  • Washed before drop-off

    If you would not hand it to a friend's kid as-is, wash it first.

  • No prop weapons, even toy ones

    Leave the toy swords at home: the Neon Demon Hunter's toy sword is a great build, but most schools ban anything weapon-shaped, even plastic.

  • No sharp bits, no broken zippers

    If it snags, pokes, or will not close, it is not rack-ready. Fix it or skip it.

  • Size label on everything

    No label, no rack. A strip of masking tape and a marker is enough.

  • One in, one out

    The rack only works if it stays stocked. Take a costume, leave a costume.

  • No selling, no holds over text

    Swaps are free and first-come. The sign-up sheet is for matching sizes, not for claiming dibs.

  • Hats, wigs, and masks get a lice check

    Shared headwear is how lice travels. Check the seams for nits before it goes on the rack, or skip the headwear and swap the rest of the costume.
