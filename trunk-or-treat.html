Spooky Graveyard Spooky

Best for: The whole family, A group of friends. Trunk build: about one evening.

The trunk

  • Cardboard tombstones from old boxes, gray paint, and marker writing.
  • A black tablecloth over the open trunk reads as fresh dirt.
  • Battery tea lights in jars, never real candles near costumes.
  • A fog machine if you own one, or a fan behind a white sheet.

Best for: Just the kids, The whole family. Trunk build: under an hour.

Trunk night checklist

  • Arrive early enough to decorate before the kids arrive.
  • Battery lights only. No real candles near costumes.
  • Bring candy plus one non-candy option for kids with allergies.
  • Make sure the kid costume fits over a jacket. Check your Halloween-night temp.
  • Charge your phone. You will take more photos than you expect.
