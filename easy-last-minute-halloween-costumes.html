The fastest build on this page: Basketball Star

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Basketball Star

Real numbers: 5 min - $3-8 - Easy

You will need:

  1. Basketball jersey or numbered tank top, 1 (own, or buy: sporting goods store)
  2. Basketball shorts, 1 pair (own)
  3. Black non-toxic face paint or 1 eyeliner pencil (buy: toy store)
  4. Basketball, 1 (own, or buy: toy store)
  1. Pull on the basketball jersey and the basketball shorts.
  2. Safety: use only the black non-toxic face paint. Skip the eye black for very young kids who will rub their eyes.
  3. Draw two short black stripes under each eye with the black non-toxic face paint. Keep the stripes short and below the eye, never on the eyelid, and skip them for kids who rub their eyes.
  4. Carry the basketball everywhere, tucked under one arm.
  5. Dribble it whenever you stand still.
  6. Optional pro finish: add a wristband and tall socks. Athletes are uniforms plus accessories; the wristband is the cheapest one.
