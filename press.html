What Pick My Costume is

Pick My Costume (pickmycostume.com) is a free Halloween costume picker for families. Answer 5 quick questions (who's dressing up, the vibe, how much effort) and get a costume pick with a materials list, build time, and step-by-step instructions.

  • 164 DIY costume guides, each with materials, steps, and timing
  • A make-tonight list that shows which costumes need zero shopping, one supply, or two
  • A "costume galaxy" that lays out all 164 ideas as an explorable star map: pickmycostume.com/map
  • No signup, no ads, no data collected beyond anonymous usage stats

Who built it

Billy, a parent in Redmond, WA. He built it as a solo side project because every October he ended up panic-scrolling for a costume the week before Halloween.
