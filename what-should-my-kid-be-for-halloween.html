The 10-minute build: Classic Ghost

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Classic Ghost

Real numbers: 10 min - Easy

You will need:

  1. 1 white flat sheet: twin size for kids under 8, full size for tweens and adults (make: use an old sheet; buy: thrift store for a dollar or two)
  2. Black marker, thick tip, 1 (buy: dollar store)
  3. Scissors, 1 pair
  4. 2 small squares of black tulle or sheer fabric, about 4 inches each (optional, for see-through eyes; buy: craft store)
  1. Pick the sheet size first: twin flat for a small kid, full for a bigger kid or adult. Bigger than you need is fine; smaller is not.
  2. Drape the sheet over the wearer and have them stand still. Mark both eye spots with the black marker while the sheet is on, marking blind never lines up.
  3. Take the sheet off. Cut the eye holes SMALL first, about the size of a quarter. You can always widen them; you cannot shrink them.
  4. Hold the sheet up and check: the wearer should see clearly through both holes. Widen a little at a time until they can.
  5. Optional pro finish: tape a square of black tulle behind each hole. From outside the eyes look dark and hollow; from inside you see straight through the mesh.
  6. Safety: trim the bottom so the hem clears the ground by several inches, a dragging hem is what trips a kid on a dark sidewalk. Keep nothing tight around the neck.
  7. Draw a wavy or smiling mouth below the eyes with the black marker. Drape it back on and go.

FAQ

What should my toddler be for Halloween?

Pick comfort over theme. A yellow sweatsuit plus a green felt stem hat makes a banana that takes 10 minutes, and the costume underneath is just pajamas your toddler already wears. If anything itches or comes off easily at this age, the night is over, so keep it to one simple piece.

What are the most popular kids Halloween costumes for 2026?

Fairy Tale Princesses rank third on the kids list, with 2.2 million kids planning it this year. The Classic Ghost is a top-five pick for kids and adults alike, and it never leaves the list. If you want something newer, the Tin Hero and the Glow Skeleton are this year's breakout kids picks, both built from the closet.

What is an easy last-minute costume for a kid?

The Classic Ghost takes 10 minutes: a white sheet, a thick black marker, and scissors. Twin flat sheet for kids under 8, full for bigger kids. Cut the eye holes small first, a quarter size, and widen slowly so your kid sees clearly through both.

How do I keep my kid warm in their Halloween costume?

Pick a costume built on a sweatsuit instead of over one. The Fuzzy Monster is a fuzzy sweatsuit with googly eyes, so thermal layers disappear underneath and there is nothing to carry. As a rule: if the costume cannot fit a fleece under it, the kid will be cold by house ten.

What should siblings dress up as together for Halloween?

Snow Sisters works for two sisters, one icy blue dress and one pink, with glitter glue, 30 minutes of build time. For a whole family, the Aussie Dog Family is blue or orange shirts plus paper ears on headbands, 25 minutes, and the grown-ups match the little ones.
