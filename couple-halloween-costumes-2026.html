1. How much time can you give it?

Be honest about effort. Both costumes take the same time, so double the one person's build in your head.

If: You have ten minutes

The pick: Office Couple

White button-downs, black pants, joke job titles written on name tags. One person carries a toy teapot all evening, the other carries a clipboard or a stack of papers.

Ten minutes, almost everything from the closet. The name tags are the only thing you might need to buy, and index cards with safety pins from home do the job.

A 10-minute duo build, word for word: Office Couple

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Office Couple

Real numbers: 10 min - Easy

You will need:

  1. White button-down shirts, 2 (own: from closet)
  2. Name tags, 2 (buy: office supply store, or make: index cards with safety pins)
  3. Toy teapot or a small real teapot, 1 (own: from the kitchen)
  4. Black pants or skirts, 2 (own: from closet)
  5. Marker for writing names, 1 (own: household)
  1. Buy name tags, or make them from index cards and safety pins. Pull the white button-down shirts and black pants or skirts from the closet.
  2. Write joke job titles on the name tags with the marker and pin them on the white button-down shirts.
  3. One person carries the toy teapot proudly all evening. The other carries a clipboard or a stack of papers.
  4. Tuck the white button-down shirts in and straighten each other's collars. Introduce yourselves by your fake titles to everyone you meet.
  5. Optional pro finish: write the fake titles in a dead-serious font, like 'Regional Teapot Manager'. The comedy is in how official the name tag looks.

What are good couples Halloween costumes for 2026?

What is an easy last-minute couples costume?

How do we make a couples costume that reads in photos?
