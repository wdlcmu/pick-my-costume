🧩 Block Party Duo

45 min + drying | $3-8 | Easy

You need

  • Two large cardboard boxes, flattened (make: from shipping boxes)
  • Acrylic paint in two bright colors, 1 set (buy: craft store)
  • Wide ribbon or webbing for shoulder straps, about 4 yards (buy: craft store)
  • Painter's tape, 1 roll (buy: dollar store)
  • Scissors or a box cutter, 1
  • Ruler or yardstick, 1 (own)

Build it (key steps)

  1. Mark a grid of 12-inch squares on the cardboard with the ruler.
  2. Each block shape is 4 squares: cut an L shape for one person and a T or square shape for the other. Cut the squares oversized first and trim after a fitting.
  3. Paint each shape its color and let dry fully, flat, for at least 2 hours.
  4. Tape ribbon loops to the top of each shape for shoulder straps. Test the hang before the party: each shape should sit at chest height.
  5. Safety: cut on a protected surface with the blade pointed away from fingers, and round the cardboard corners so nobody gets poked in a crowd.
