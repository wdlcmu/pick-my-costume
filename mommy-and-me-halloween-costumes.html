The full build: Beekeeper & Bee

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Beekeeper & Bee

Real numbers: 25 min - Easy

You will need:

  1. White long-sleeve shirt and white pants, 1 set (own, or buy: thrift store)
  2. Fine mesh fabric or a mosquito head net, 1 (buy: craft store or camping aisle)
  3. Wide-brim hat, 1 (own, or buy: dollar store)
  4. Yellow shirt and yellow pants, 1 set (own, or buy: thrift store)
  5. Black electrical tape, 1 roll (buy: dollar store)
  6. Black pipe cleaners for antennae, 2 (buy: craft store)
  7. Headband, 1 (buy: dollar store, or use your own)
  8. Yellow and black face paint sticks, 1 set (buy: craft store)
  9. Scissors, 1 pair
  1. Beekeeper: drape the mesh fabric over the wide-brim hat so it hangs past the shoulders, and tie it at the neck.
  2. Cut the face opening wide first, you can trim it neater after the first fitting.
  3. Bee: wrap 3 to 4 black tape stripes around the yellow shirt and pants, evenly spaced. Press the tape flat so the edges do not peel.
  4. Bend the pipe cleaners into a V and tape them to the headband for antennae. Add two black face-paint dots on the cheeks.
  5. Safety: cut the veil opening wide enough that vision stays clear, and tie nothing tight around the neck. Use tape, not string, for the stripes so nothing unravels.
  6. The adult is the beekeeper: white outfit with the veil hat, since the mesh limits vision. The smaller of the two is the bee: stripes with the antennae headband.
  7. Optional pro finish: the beekeeper carries a small cardboard sign that says HONEY, and the bee poses mid-buzz next to it. One shared prop is what makes a pair read as a pair.

FAQ

Do I really not have to build my own costume?

For the lifeguard pair, no: the beachgoer is a sundress and sunglasses. For the others, the mom side is one closet outfit plus one small piece: a shaker top, a veil, or a bottle cap.

What if my kid will not wear their half?

Pick the pair where the kid half builds on their normal clothes: the bee stripes go on a yellow shirt they already wear, and the lifeguard is a red tee. Nothing new touching the skin is the rule.

How much do these cost?

Salt and Pepper, Ketchup and Mustard, Beekeeper and Bee, Little Lifeguard: every guide lists the build time and the full materials list, closet items first.

Can we build these the night before?

Yes. The longest is Beekeeper and Bee at 25 minutes. Salt and Pepper needs drying time for the shaker tops, so build those first.
