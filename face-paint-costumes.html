Face Paint Costume Ideas

When the costume is mostly face paint, the outfit can be dead simple, black clothes plus a painted skeleton face beats a store-bought suit every time.

Use proper face paint or makeup, not craft paint or markers, and do a small patch test on the jaw an hour before in case of sensitive skin. Paint the base first, let it set, then add details, and keep makeup wipes in your pocket because touch-ups are part of the deal.

Every costume below leans on the paint job, lists the rest honestly, and links to a full build guide with real hands-on time. The brush is the costume.

Basketball Star

⏱ 5 min hands-onEasy

You need: Basketball jersey or numbered tank top, 1; Basketball shorts, 1 pair; Black non-toxic face paint or 1 eyeliner pencil; Basketball, 1.

Jersey, shorts, eye-black stripes, and a ball that never leaves your hand.

How to make it

  1. Pull on the basketball jersey and shorts.
  2. Draw two short black stripes under each eye with non-toxic face paint. Keep the stripes below the eye, never on the eyelid, and skip them for very young kids who will rub their eyes.
  3. Carry the basketball tucked under one arm, and dribble it whenever you stand still.

Ninja

⏱ 10 min hands-onEasy

You need: All-black outfit: t-shirt, pants, and a black beanie, 1 set; Black belt or sash, 1; Black headband or strip of black t-shirt, 1 strip about 3 inches wide; Scissors, 1 pair, and tape, 1 roll; Black non-toxic face paint for a mask stripe, 1 tube.

All black with a belt sash and a slit headband.

How to make it

  1. Dress all in black and tie the sash around the waist.
  2. Wrap the black strip around the forehead, with eyes and mouth uncovered.
  3. Paint a thin black stripe across the nose if you like.

Boxer

⏱ 10 min hands-onEasy

You need: Bathrobe, 1; Toy boxing gloves, 1 pair; Purple and yellow non-toxic face paint, 1 tube each; Athletic shorts and sneakers, 1 set; Phone for entrance music, 1.

Bathrobe, toy gloves, bruise makeup, and entrance music.

How to make it

  1. Pull on the athletic shorts, sneakers, and bathrobe, with the hood up.
  2. Dab purple and yellow non-toxic face paint on one cheekbone, below one eye, never on the eyelid. One bruise only, and keep all paint clear of the eyes.
  3. Pull on the toy boxing gloves, throw a few shadow punches, and play entrance music as you walk in.

Gloom & Bloom

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 set black clothes from the closet; 1 set colorful clothes from the closet; Black and white face paint, non-toxic, 1 set; 2 hair ties for braids; 3 to 5 fake flowers.

Braids and black for Gloom, color-pop and smiles for Bloom.

How to make it

  1. Gloom wears the black clothes and tight braids, and paints a small black teardrop under one eye. Keep the paint clear of the eyes, and test it on skin first.
  2. Bloom wears the colorful clothes, pins the fake flowers in the hair, and paints a small flower on one cheek, clear of the eyes.
  3. Stand side by side: Gloom frowns, Bloom smiles big.

Mystery Crew

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Orange, purple, blue, and red t-shirts, 1 each; Dog ears headband or a brown dog costume piece, 1; Fake magnifying glass, 1; Headband or scarf in a matching color, 1 per person; Brown non-toxic face paint for the dog's nose, 1 tube.

Assign the leader, the style icon, the brains, the goofball, and one very good dog.

How to make it

  1. Assign the roles: orange for the leader, purple for the style icon, blue for the brains, red for the goofball. The dog wears the ears headband.
  2. Each person puts on their color t-shirt and a matching headband or scarf, tied loose, never tight. The brains carries the fake magnifying glass.
  3. Paint a brown nose on the dog with non-toxic face paint, keeping it clear of the eyes. Line up with the dog in front, and point at something suspicious.

Deadpan Diva

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Black dress, 1; Very pale foundation or white non-toxic face paint, 1; Dark eyeliner and lipstick, 1 set; 2 hair ties; Thin black ribbon, 1 short length (optional, for the choker; buy: craft store).

Black dress, two braids, pale makeup, and a stare that ends conversations.

How to make it

  1. Braid the hair into two tight braids. Test the pale foundation on the jawline first, then put it on evenly and blend it into the neck.
  2. Add the dark eyeliner and lipstick, and put on the black dress. Use skin-safe makeup only, and wash it all off before bed.
  3. Fold the arms and hold the stare. Add reflective tape or a glow stick, and carry a flashlight for visibility at night.

Cat & Mouse

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: 2 headbands; 1 sheet black craft felt, 9x12 inches; 1 sheet gray craft felt, 9x12 inches; Black eyeliner pencil or non-toxic face paint, 1; Scissors, 1 pair; Tape, 1 roll; Pink felt, 1 small triangle (optional, for inner ears; buy: craft store).

Cat ears versus mouse ears. Spend the night chasing each other.

How to make it

  1. Cut a paper ear first to test the size: triangles for the cat, rounds for the mouse. Then cut two triangle ears from the black felt and two round ears from the gray felt.
  2. Tape one pair of ears to each headband, and put the headbands on.
  3. Draw whiskers on both faces with the eyeliner pencil, keeping the eyeliner clear of the eyes. Spend the night chasing each other.

Plumber Duo

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Blue overalls, 2; Red t-shirt and green t-shirt, 1 each; Red cap and green cap, 1 each; Brown non-toxic face paint for mustaches, 1 tube; White circle stickers or white paper, 2 about 2 inches across; Marker, 1.

Overalls, red and green caps and shirts, drawn mustaches.

How to make it

  1. Write a big M on one white circle and an L on the other, and stick or tape them to the red cap and the green cap.
  2. Each brother wears blue overalls over their colored t-shirt and cap.
  3. Paint on the mustaches with brown non-toxic face paint: a big bushy one for red, a thinner one for green. Keep the paint clear of the eyes, and test it on skin first.

Little Witch

Good Witch, Bad Witch

⏱ 20 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 black dress or set of black clothes for the bad witch; 1 pink dress or set of pink clothes for the good witch; Green face paint, non-toxic, 1 tube; 1 cardboard crown; 1 black cone hat; Hot glue gun, scissors, 1 pink marker, tape.

Green face paint and black for one, pink gown and crown for the other.

How to make it

  1. Bad witch: wear black, and paint the face and hands green with non-toxic paint. Keep the paint away from the eyes, and patch test sensitive skin first.
  2. An adult folds the black hat brim and hot glues it in place. Hot glue sets in seconds, so check the fit in a mirror first.
  3. Good witch: wear pink, color the cardboard crown with the pink marker, and tape it on.

Questions, answered

Can I use craft paint or markers on skin?

No. Use proper face paint or makeup, and do a small patch test on the jaw an hour before in case of sensitive skin. Craft paint can irritate and stain.

What order do I paint in?

Base first, let it set, then details. Details painted onto wet base bleed; details painted onto set base stay crisp.

How do I keep it from smearing all night?

Keep makeup wipes in your pocket because touch-ups are part of the deal, and set the finished paint with a light dusting of translucent powder.
