What is an easy last-minute Halloween costume?

The Classic Ghost takes 10 minutes and $3 to $8 of materials. The Deadpan Diva takes 15 minutes and $3 to $8. Cat and Mouse takes 15 minutes and $3 to $6. All three read instantly at any party.

What should a couple be for Halloween?

Pick a pair with built-in contrast: Cat and Mouse (15 minutes, $3 to $6), Sun and Moon (40 minutes, $5 to $8), The Doctor and the Bride (25 minutes, $5 to $12), or Rain Cloud and Rainbow (30 minutes, $4 to $10).

What is a good family costume with kids?

The Aussie Dog Family, Superhero Family, Blue Alien Ohana, and Little Pig Family all work from toddlers to grown-ups, each takes 25 to 30 minutes, and each costs $3 to $10 in materials.
