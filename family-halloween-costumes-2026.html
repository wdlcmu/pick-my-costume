The full build: Mystery Crew

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Mystery Crew

Real numbers: 15 min - $2-6 - Easy

You will need:

  1. Orange, purple, blue, and red t-shirts, 1 each (buy: thrift store, or own: from closet)
  2. Dog ears headband or a brown dog costume piece, 1 (buy: dollar store)
  3. Fake magnifying glass, 1 (buy: dollar store, or make: a paper circle taped to a stick)
  4. Headband or scarf in a matching color, 1 per person (own: from the drawer)
  5. Brown non-toxic face paint for the dog's nose, 1 tube (buy: drugstore)
  1. Assign roles: orange leader, purple style icon, blue brains, red goofball, and the dog wears the ears.
  2. Each person: put on their color's t-shirt and tie a matching headband or scarf.
  3. The brains carries the fake magnifying glass. Paint a brown nose on the dog with the brown face paint.
  4. Group the four humans in a line with the dog in front. All point at something suspicious for the classic pose.
  5. Optional pro finish: write each person's role on a small paper name tag ('the brains', 'the leader'). The tags turn colored shirts into characters for anyone who does not know the reference.

FAQ

How do we get everyone to agree on one theme?

Narrow to 3 options, then let each person veto one. Three vetoes and one theme left is how families actually decide. The quiz on the homepage does the narrowing part: 5 questions, 3 ideas.

What if the toddler refuses to wear the costume?

Build it on clothes they already wear. A hoodie costume like the Baby Dinosaur goes over their normal outfit, so nothing new touches their skin. Skip face paint for refusal-prone toddlers.

How much will matching family costumes cost?

The group picks on this page run $2 to $15 per person: Mystery Crew is $2 to $6, Emoji Crew $5 to $12, the Olympians $8 to $15. Every number comes from the build guides, materials included.

Can we build these in one evening?

Mystery Crew and Emoji Crew are 15-minute builds. The Olympians take 45 minutes for the togas and crowns. Start with a fast one and add the bigger build only if the evening is going well.
