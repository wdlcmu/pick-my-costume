About

Why this site exists, in about a minute.

I'm a dad who burned a whole evening scrolling costume ideas for my kid and ended up with nothing. Every list was the same: store links, sponsored picks, and "easy DIY" projects that needed a sewing machine and three weekends.

So I had AI build this quiz instead. You answer a few questions about who the costume is for, the vibe, and how much effort you'll spend. It picks 3 ideas from a bank of 137 original costumes, each with a materials list and numbered build steps. Every idea says what it costs and how long it takes before you click.

The rules I built it on: no licensed characters (you won't find Elsa here, but you'll find the Snow Sisters), no store links pretending to be ideas, no account, no ads, no signup. The guides are written for parents building with or for kids, because that's who I was when I needed this.

How it's made

The site is a static page with a hand-built bank of 137 costume ideas. The quiz matches your answers to the bank; nothing leaves your browser. I keep adding ideas and improving the guides, and I dogfood every change with my own family before it ships.

Say hello

Found a broken step, a bad materials list, or a costume your kid loved? I'd genuinely like to hear it: hello@pickmycostume.com
