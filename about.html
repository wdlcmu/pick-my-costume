What this is

A free Halloween costume picker. A few quick questions, one top pick, no account.

Answer a few quick questions. Get your top costume pick, plus two runners-up, each with a full step-by-step build guide. For yourself, your kid, your partner, your group, or the whole family.

Who's behind this

I'm Billy, a parent in Redmond, Washington. I built Pick My Costume for my 3-year-old, because every October I watched parents (myself included) panic-buy costumes the week before Halloween, when the good ones were already sold out or overpriced. Every costume on this site is written against a real materials list — if it says you need a cardboard box and paint, that's what you need.

The photos are AI-generated concept images to show what each costume looks like, except where marked as a real build photo. When I photograph a real costume I've built, I'll label it.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no email, no ads. Your answers never leave your phone; we only track anonymous usage stats.

What is the Costume Galaxy?

It’s all 169 costume ideas as an explorable map at /map/ — nine worlds like Dinosaur Land and Food Court. Zoom out to see the regions, zoom in for photos, tap any costume for its build guide.

What does “I’m from Dinosaur Land” mean?

When you finish the quiz, your top pick’s home region becomes your region — the Sorting Hat model. That’s what the share line means, and the map will have your region picked out next time you visit.

How does the quiz pick my costume?

Your answers are scored against a hand-picked bank of 169 costume ideas. You get a top pick plus two runners-up, each with a full step-by-step build guide. The Surprise Me and Wildcard buttons are the exception: those picks are random on purpose.

Something wrong or a costume we should add?

Email hello@pickmycostume.com — a human reads it.
