      How this works

      Does this work if last year's costume was store-bought?

      If it was mostly store-bought, there are no homemade parts to match, and the page will tell you so honestly. It works best with DIY builds: capes, wings, face paint, cardboard props, and costumes made from old clothes.

      What counts as a matching part?

      Exact matches are the same material, like green felt to green felt. Same-kind matches are the same material in a different color, like green paper to red paper. Those are shown separately because you will still need the new color. Craft-drawer basics like scissors and tape are never counted.

      Can I pass a costume down to a younger sibling instead?

      Yes. The parts do not care who wears them. Pick the older kid's costume above and the ranking works the same. Check the build guide for sizing notes before you start.
