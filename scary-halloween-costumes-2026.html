The full-horror build on this page: Classic Vampire

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Classic Vampire

Real numbers: 25 min - Easy

You will need:

  1. Black cape or black bedsheet, 1 (buy cape: costume aisle; or make: from a black sheet from home)
  2. Black shirt and pants, 1 set (own: from closet)
  3. Plastic vampire fangs, 1 pair (buy: costume aisle or dollar store)
  4. Hair gel or pomade, 1 (own: from home)
  5. Red lipstick or red face paint, 1 (buy: pharmacy or costume aisle)
  6. White face powder or pale face paint, 1 (buy: costume aisle)
  1. Dress in the black shirt and pants.
  2. Make the hair: work the hair gel through the hair with your fingers. Press it straight back until it sits flat and slick.
  3. Safety: use non-toxic costume makeup, and skip the vampire fangs for small kids who might choke on them.
  4. Make the face: dust the pale face paint over the face and neck. Dab a thin line of red lipstick at each corner of the mouth like drips of blood.
  5. Wear the black cape over the shoulders and pop the vampire fangs in last before photos.
  6. Optional pro finish: paint the nails black and pop the cape collar up high, since the collar silhouette is what reads as vampire from across the room.
