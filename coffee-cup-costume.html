Coffee Cup Costume

The whole coffee cup build is on this page: a white sheet or large white bag, a cardboard tube, and a brown paper lid, in 20 minutes. It reads at ten feet, and it pairs naturally with a donut. Below it are nine more breakfast builds for one person, a pair, or a group, each with its own photo, hands-on time, supply list, and guide.

Start here: Coffee Cup, the full build

For a child under eight, follow the guide’s safety swap and use a white sheet, not a trash bag.

⏱ 20 minEasy

White trash bag over a cardboard tube, brown lid hat.

What you need

  • White trash bag or white sheet, 1 large (make: from home)
  • Cardboard tube or large cylinder, 1 (make: from home)
  • Brown paper or felt for the lid, 1 sheet (buy: craft store)
  • Brown marker, 1 thick (buy: dollar store)

Build it, step by step

  1. Cut the arm holes first.
  2. Then cut the head hole small.
  3. Hold the bag up to test the size. Never pull it over the head.
  4. Cut the holes in the white trash bag for head and arms.
  5. Safety: Cut the head hole wide enough. The wearer must see and breathe with ease. Wear the bag over clothes, never on bare skin. Use a white sheet for kids under 8. Take it off if you feel hot.
  6. This is the cup.
  7. A head hole cut too big slides off the shoulders.
  8. Draw a coffee shop logo on the front with brown marker.
  9. Make the lid: cut a brown paper circle bigger than your head, with a sip hole.
  10. Wear the bag.
  11. Put the lid hat on top.
  12. Optional pro finish: cut a cardboard sleeve, write a coffee-shop pun on it, and tape it around the torso.

Questions, answered

What should a child use instead of a trash bag?

A white sheet. The Coffee Cup guide says to use a white sheet for kids under 8, wear it over clothes, cut the head hole wide enough for easy seeing and breathing, and take it off if the wearer feels hot.

Do any of these need sewing?

No build needs stitching. They use cutting, tape, safety pins, glue, and paint. The Milk & Cookies list mentions a sewing kit only as a place to find its four safety pins.

Can I make the coffee cup as a pair?

Yes. Donut & Coffee is the built pair on this page: one person wears the pink frosted ring and the other wears the takeout cup. Bacon & Eggs, Peanut Butter & Jelly, and Milk & Cookies are also two-person builds.

How long does the lead build take?

20 minutes of hands-on time and no drying time. Six of the alternatives name drying time for paint or glue, from 20 minutes to an hour.
