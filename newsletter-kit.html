Tips for the best placement

  • Send it in your early-October newsletter. Most people pick costumes in the first two weeks of October.
  • Put it near the top. Seasonal content is what residents actually read.
  • The links carry a newsletter tag so we can count how many neighbors found a costume. No names, no individual tracking.
