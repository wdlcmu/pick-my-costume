Family Halloween Costume Ideas

A family costume only works if the littlest member can actually wear it, sit in it, and survive the evening in it.

The sets below were picked because every member gets a real costume, not a background role, and the builds scale: make the baby the centerpiece, give the teens the funny parts, and keep total build time sane by repeating one simple base across the group. Start with the hardest costume first, usually the smallest person, and fit everything while they are still in a good mood.

Every set below links to full build guides with real hands-on time per person, so there are no day-of surprises.

Goggle Crew

⏱ 10 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 yellow t-shirt per person; 1 pair denim overalls per person; 1 pair swim or safety goggles per person; 1 pair black gloves per person.

Yellow tees, denim overalls, goggles, black gloves.

How to make it

  1. Each person puts on a yellow t-shirt, denim overalls, and black gloves.
  2. Push the goggles up on the forehead, never over the eyes, so everyone can see clearly.
  3. Walk in a pack, and greet people in gibberish. Skip any real tools or hard hats.

Classic Ghost

⏱ 10 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 white flat sheet: twin size for kids under 8, full size for tweens and adults; Black marker, thick tip, 1; Scissors, 1 pair; 2 small squares of black tulle or sheer fabric, about 4 inches each (optional, for see-through eyes; buy: craft store).

A white sheet with cut-out eyes.

How to make it

  1. Drape the sheet over the wearer and mark both eye spots.
  2. Take the sheet off. Cut the holes small first, then widen a little at a time.
  3. Draw a mouth, trim the hem so it clears the ground, and drape the sheet back on.

Emoji Crew

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 yellow t-shirt per person; 1 large white paper plate, 10-inch, or cardboard circle per person; Markers in black, red, and blue, 1 set; Scissors, 1 pair and tape, 1 roll.

Everyone picks an emoji: a yellow tee plus a big printed face.

How to make it

  1. Everyone picks an emoji, and puts on a yellow t-shirt.
  2. Draw the emoji on a paper plate in pencil first, then go over it with the markers. An adult handles the scissors when trimming the plate.
  3. Tape the plate flat to the chest of the shirt, and gather the crew in a row for photos.

Dino Rangers

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Khaki shirt, 1, and khaki pants, 1 pair, per grown-up; Green hoodie per child, 1 each; White paper or card, 2 sheets; 1 toy dinosaur or plush per kid; Ribbon or string, 18 inches per kid; Tape, 1 roll and scissors, 1 pair.

Khaki outfits for the grown-ups, dino hoods for the kids. Leash a toy raptor.

How to make it

  1. Grown-ups dress in khaki shirts and pants, with the sleeves rolled. Each kid wears a green hoodie.
  2. An adult draws two oval eye spots on white paper, checks them against the hood, and cuts them out. Tape the spots to the hood.
  3. Loop the ribbon around a toy dinosaur like a leash, and tie the other end to the kid's wrist, keeping the leash short. Skip small plastic dinosaur toys for kids under three, since they are a choking hazard.

Bowling Pins

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: White shirt and pants per pin person, 1 set each; Red duct or electrical tape, 1 roll; Black shirt and pants for the bowler, 1 set; Toy bowling ball or black playground ball, 1; Scissors, 1 pair.

White outfits with red neck stripes, plus one bowler in black.

How to make it

  1. Wrap two red tape stripes around the collar of the white shirt.
  2. Wrap one stripe around each wrist.
  3. The bowler dresses in black and carries a toy ball, never a real one.

Mystery Crew

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Orange, purple, blue, and red t-shirts, 1 each; Dog ears headband or a brown dog costume piece, 1; Fake magnifying glass, 1; Headband or scarf in a matching color, 1 per person; Brown non-toxic face paint for the dog's nose, 1 tube.

Assign the leader, the style icon, the brains, the goofball, and one very good dog.

How to make it

  1. Assign the roles: orange for the leader, purple for the style icon, blue for the brains, red for the goofball. The dog wears the ears headband.
  2. Each person puts on their color t-shirt and a matching headband or scarf, tied loose, never tight. The brains carries the fake magnifying glass.
  3. Paint a brown nose on the dog with non-toxic face paint, keeping it clear of the eyes. Line up with the dog in front, and point at something suspicious.

Fairy Tale Princesses

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: 1 dress from the closet per person; 1 cereal box per crown; Gold or silver paint or markers, 1 set; Scissors, 1 pair and tape, 1 roll; 1 hair ribbon per person.

A dress or a crown from the closet. Every princess works.

How to make it

  1. Each princess picks a dress from her own closet.
  2. An adult draws a crown on a cereal box, checks the fit against the head, and cuts it out, leaving a 1 inch tab to tape the ends together.
  3. Color the crown gold or silver, tape it on, and tie the hair ribbon in a bow under the crown.

Plumber Duo

⏱ 15 min hands-onEasy

You need: Blue overalls, 2; Red t-shirt and green t-shirt, 1 each; Red cap and green cap, 1 each; Brown non-toxic face paint for mustaches, 1 tube; White circle stickers or white paper, 2 about 2 inches across; Marker, 1.

Overalls, red and green caps and shirts, drawn mustaches.

How to make it

  1. Write a big M on one white circle and an L on the other, and stick or tape them to the red cap and the green cap.
  2. Each brother wears blue overalls over their colored t-shirt and cap.
  3. Paint on the mustaches with brown non-toxic face paint: a big bushy one for red, a thinner one for green. Keep the paint clear of the eyes, and test it on skin first.

Cereal Crew

⏱ 20 min hands-onEasy

You need: Solid-color shirt and pants per person, 1 set each; Empty cereal boxes, 1 per person; Markers or crayons in assorted colors, 1 pack; String, about 4 feet per person; Scissors, 1 pair; Tape, 1 roll.

Solid-color clothes plus a cereal-box front you decorate.

How to make it

  1. Cut the front panel off an empty cereal box.
  2. Decorate it with a made-up cereal name and mascot.
  3. Tape string to the top corners and wear the panel like a sign.

Decades Crew

⏱ 20 min hands-onEasy

You need: Each person's own closet clothes; 1 printed decade card per person, e.g. 70s, 80s, 90s, cardstock or paper; Scissors, 1 pair; Tape, 1 roll or safety pins, 1 pack; Hair gel or spray, 1.

Each person picks a decade and dresses from their own closet.

How to make it

  1. Everyone picks a different decade, and builds the look from their own closet: bell bottoms and a headband for the 70s, neon and high socks for the 80s, flannel tied at the waist for the 90s.
  2. Letter each decade card big, with letters at least 4 inches tall, and tape one card to each chest. An adult handles the scissors when trimming the cards.
  3. Style the hair with gel to match the decade, and keep props light so the looks last all night.

Mermaid Crew

⏱ 20 min hands-onEasy

You need: T-shirt or dress in a sea color (teal, purple, red, white), 1 per person; Face paint in a matching accent color, 1 tube; Plastic trident prop, 1; Toy crab or red paper for a cardboard crab, 1; Shell necklace, 1; Scissors and tape, 1 set.

The mermaid, the prince, the sea king, the sea witch, or the crab: pick your role.

How to make it

  1. Sketch the cheek detail on paper first, then paint one small detail on each person: scales, a seaweed swirl, or an anchor. Keep the face paint clear of the eyes.
  2. Cut a red paper crab body, legs, and claws slowly, and tape them to the smallest person's shirt, or hand them the toy crab instead.
  3. The sea king carries the plastic trident. Everyone else picks one accessory, like the shell necklace, and stands tall for the crew photo.

Fruit Salad Crew

⏱ 20 min hands-onEasy

You need: Solid-color outfits in fruit colors (red, yellow, green, orange), 1 set per person; Green felt for leaf hats, 1 sheet 9x12 inches per person; Headbands, 1 per person; Fabric glue, 1 tube.

Each person picks a fruit color, wears it head to toe with a leaf hat.

How to make it

  1. Each person picks a fruit, and wears that color head to toe.
  2. An adult cuts leaf shapes from the green felt, keeping fingers clear of the scissors.
  3. Glue one leaf to each headband and press it down firmly. Wear the headbands, and take the fruit salad group photo.

Questions, answered

Which family member's costume should we build first?

The smallest. Fit the toddler or baby while they are still in a good mood, then scale the adult versions off the same simple base.

Do we all have to match exactly?

No. One repeated color, hat, or prop reads as a group in photos. Matching exactly multiplies the work; a shared element is enough.

How do we keep total build time sane?

Repeat one simple base across the group instead of building six different costumes. Assembly-line the repeated piece, then add one distinguishing detail per person.
