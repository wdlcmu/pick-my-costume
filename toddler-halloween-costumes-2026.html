The full build: Baby Dinosaur

If Halloween is closer than you think, here is the whole build, word for word from the costume guide.

The pick: Baby Dinosaur

Real numbers: 20 min - $3-8 - Easy

You will need:

  1. Green hoodie, 1 (buy: clothing store, or use your own)
  2. 2 sheets green craft felt, 9x12 inches each (buy: craft store, construction paper works too)
  3. Pillow stuffing or 2 old socks (make: stuff from home)
  4. Safety pins, 6 to 8 (buy: dollar store)
  5. Masking tape, 1 roll
  6. Scissors, 1 pair
  1. Cut a paper triangle template first and test the size on the hoodie.
  2. Then cut 6 to 8 triangle spikes from the green craft felt with the scissors, spikes cut too small look lost on the back, so go palm-size.
  3. Tape the spikes in a row down the back of the green hoodie with the masking tape. Pin each spike from inside the hoodie with a safety pin.
  4. Stuff the old socks with the pillow stuffing and tape the open ends shut with the masking tape.
  5. Safety: pin everything from inside the green hoodie, so pin backs face the fabric and never the skin.
  6. Pin the stuffed tail to the back waistband of the green hoodie.
  7. Optional pro finish: cut two small white felt triangles and pin them to the hoodie cuffs as claws. Claws sell the dinosaur more than the spikes do.

FAQ

Will my toddler actually keep the costume on?

The ones built on normal clothes win. A hoodie or sweatsuit costume adds almost nothing new touching their skin, which is where most costume rejections start. Avoid masks and hats they did not choose.

Face paint or skip it?

Skip it for under-twos and any kid who rubs their face. Every pick on this page reads clearly without paint: the Little Witch works in just the cape and hat.

What about the stroller?

Hoodie and sweatsuit costumes buckle in fine. Skip wide wings, trailing capes, and anything that bunches behind them in the seat.

How warm are these?

Most are a single layer over normal clothes, which is exactly right for an October evening. The Fuzzy Monster sweatsuit is the warmest of the bunch.
