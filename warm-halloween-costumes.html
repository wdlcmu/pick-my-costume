Questions, answered

How do you keep a costume warm without ruining it?

Build the costume one size loose and put the warm layer under it, not over it. A coat under a cloak still reads as a cloak; a coat over a costume hides it. Thermals under a sweatsuit disappear completely.

What is the warmest costume on this page?

The Fuzzy Monster and the Penguin Huddle. Both are full sweatsuits with hoods, so they take a thermal layer and gloves with nothing showing. The Astronaut covers head to toe as well.

Can these work for toddlers?

Yes. The Little Lion and the Penguin Huddle are built for small kids, with hoods instead of masks and room for a warm layer under the costume on the coldest nights.

Do I need to sew anything?

No. These builds use tape, glue, and safety pins. Where a seam would help, the guide offers a pin or tape fix that lasts the night.
