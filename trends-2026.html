Where these numbers come from

Counts come straight from our live idea bank of 164 costumes: audience, budget, and vibe tags on every idea. The top 5 come from a demand simulation: 100,000 quiz runs against the same bank, counting which idea lands at number one. Percentages are shares of those simulated runs. This is our own data, and we say so plainly.
